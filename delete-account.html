Promptory hisobini o'chirish

Ishlab chiquvchi: Umedjon Karayev · Ilova: Promptory (uz.promptory.app)

1-usul: ilova ichida (tavsiya etiladi)

  1. Promptory ilovasini oching va hisobingizga kiring.
  2. Profil → Sozlamalar (⚙) → Hisob va xavfsizlik bo'limiga o'ting.
  3. Hisobni o'chirish tugmasini bosing va tasdiqlash uchun foydalanuvchi nomingizni yozing.

2-usul: email orqali

Ilovaga kira olmasangiz, hisobingizga bog'langan email manzildan karayev.umedjon@gmail.com manziliga "Promptory hisobini o'chirish" mavzusida xat yuboring. So'rov 7 kun ichida bajariladi.

Nimalar o'chiriladi

Email va parol, profil (foydalanuvchi nomi, ism, bio, rasm), siz qo'shgan barcha promptlar va namuna rasmlar, yuborgan shikoyatlaringiz — darhol va butunlay. Hech qanday ma'lumot saqlab qolinmaydi. Qurilmangizdagi sevimlilar ilovani o'chirganingizda o'chadi.

Delete your Promptory account

Developer: Umedjon Karayev · App: Promptory (uz.promptory.app)

Option 1: in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Promptory and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings (⚙) → Account & security.
  3. Tap Delete account and type your username to confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot access the app, email karayev.umedjon@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my Promptory account". Requests are completed within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your email and password, profile (username, name, bio, photo), all prompts and example images you submitted, and reports you sent — immediately and permanently. No data is retained. Favorites stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.
