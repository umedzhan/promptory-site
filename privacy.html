Ushbu siyosat Promptory mobil ilovasi qanday ma'lumotlarni to'plashi, ulardan qanday foydalanishi va ularni qanday o'chirish mumkinligini tushuntiradi. Ilova egasi: Umedjon Karayev. Aloqa: karayev.umedjon@gmail.com.

1. Hisobsiz foydalanish

Promptlarni ko'rish, to'ldirish, nusxalash va sevimlilarga qo'shish uchun hisob kerak emas. Sevimlilar, yaqinda ko'rilgan promptlar va mavzu sozlamasi faqat qurilmangizda saqlanadi va bizga yuborilmaydi.

3. AI'da sinab ko'rish (Plus va Pro)

"Ilovada sinab ko'rish" yoki "Rasm yaratish" tugmasini bossangiz, to'ldirilgan prompt matni OpenAI (AQSh) xizmatiga javob olish uchun yuboriladi. OpenAI API orqali yuborilgan ma'lumotlarni o'z modellarini o'qitish uchun ishlatmaydi. Biz prompt matnini va AI javobini saqlamaymiz; faqat kunlik limit uchun vaqt, tur (matn/rasm), model va token sonini qayd etamiz. AI javobiga shikoyat qilsangiz, shikoyat bilan birga prompt va javob matni moderatorga saqlanadi.

Rasm asosida yaratish (Pro): o'zingiz tanlagan rasm (masalan, yuzingiz tasviri) faqat natija yaratish uchun OpenAI'ga yuboriladi. U bizning serverimizda saqlanmaydi va faqat so'rov davomida xotirada turadi. Rasm ham xavfsizlik tekshiruvidan (moderatsiya) o'tadi. Biz yuzni tanish yoki biometrik ma'lumot olish uchun rasmlardan foydalanmaymiz.

3.1. Xato hisobotlari

Ilova kutilmagan xatoga uchrasa, bizga xato matni, texnik iz (stack trace), ilova versiyasi va platforma (Android) yuboriladi; hisobingizga kirgan bo'lsangiz, foydalanuvchi ID ham. Bu faqat xatolarni tuzatish uchun ishlatiladi.

4. Biz nimalarni to'plamaymiz

Joylashuv, kontaktlar, qurilmadagi fayllar (siz o'zingiz tanlagan rasmdan tashqari), reklama identifikatori, analitika yoki kuzatuv ma'lumotlari to'planmaydi. Ilovada reklama yo'q. Ma'lumotlaringiz sotilmaydi va reklama maqsadida uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi.

5. Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Hisob ma'lumotlari va siz qo'shgan kontent Supabase bulut xizmatida saqlanadi. Supabase ma'lumotlarni faqat biz nomimizdan, xizmat ko'rsatish uchun qayta ishlaydi. Barcha ulanishlar HTTPS (TLS) orqali shifrlanadi. Ma'lumotlar bazasidagi ruxsat qoidalari har bir foydalanuvchi faqat o'z ma'lumotlarini o'zgartira olishini ta'minlaydi.

6. Kontent va moderatsiya

Siz qo'shgan promptlar chop etilishidan oldin moderator tomonidan tekshiriladi. Qoidalarni buzgan kontent (spam, haqorat, noqonuniy yoki xavfli material, mualliflik huquqi buzilishi) rad etiladi yoki o'chiriladi. Foydalanuvchilar istalgan promptga ilova ichida shikoyat qilishi va muallifni bloklashi mumkin. Batafsil: Foydalanish shartlari.

7. Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Hisobingizni istalgan vaqtda ilova ichida o'chirishingiz mumkin: Profil → Sozlamalar → Hisob va xavfsizlik → Hisobni o'chirish. Bunda profilingiz, rasmlaringiz, promptlaringiz va shikoyatlaringiz darhol va butunlay o'chiriladi. Ilovaga kira olmasangiz, bu yerdagi yo'riqnoma bo'yicha so'rov yuboring. Alohida promptni ilovada "Mening promptlarim" bo'limidan o'chirish mumkin.

8. Bolalar

Promptory 13 yoshdan kichik bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va ulardan ataylab ma'lumot to'plamaydi.

9. O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, yangi versiya shu sahifada sanasi bilan e'lon qilinadi.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · App: Promptory (uz.promptory.app)

This policy explains what data the Promptory mobile app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it. App owner: Umedjon Karayev. Contact: karayev.umedjon@gmail.com.

1. Using the app without an account

Browsing, filling in, copying and favoriting prompts does not require an account. Favorites, recently viewed prompts and the theme setting are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Public data: your username, name, bio, profile photo and approved prompts are visible to all app users. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

3. Try in app with AI (Plus and Pro)

When you tap "Try in app" or "Generate image", the filled-in prompt text is sent to OpenAI (USA) to generate a response. OpenAI does not use data sent via its API to train its models. We do not store the prompt text or the AI response; we only record the time, type (text/image), model and token counts for daily limits. If you report an AI response, the prompt and response are stored with the report for moderators.

Generating from your photo (Pro): a photo you choose (for example, of your face) is sent to OpenAI only to generate the result. It is not stored on our servers and stays in memory only for the duration of the request. The photo is also checked by moderation. We do not use photos for face recognition or to derive biometric data.

3.1. Error reports

If the app hits an unexpected error, it sends us the error message, technical trace (stack trace), app version and platform (Android), plus your user ID if you are signed in. This is used only to fix bugs.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect location, contacts, device files (other than images you explicitly choose), advertising IDs, analytics or tracking data. The app has no ads. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

5. Where data is stored

Account data and your content are stored with Supabase, which processes it only on our behalf to provide the service. All connections are encrypted with HTTPS (TLS). Database access rules ensure that each user can modify only their own data.

6. Content and moderation

Prompts you submit are reviewed by a moderator before publication. Content that violates the rules (spam, abuse, illegal or harmful material, copyright infringement) is rejected or removed. Users can report any prompt and block any author in the app. Details: Terms of Use.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Account & security → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, photos, prompts and reports. If you cannot access the app, follow the instructions here. Individual prompts can be deleted under "My prompts".

8. Children

Promptory is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its effective date.
